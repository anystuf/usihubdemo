## Risk Level Rubric (High/Medium/Low)

**Definition**: What obstacles could prevent success?

### Risk Dimensions

#### 1. **Product & Validation Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- No user testing or feedback collected
- Unclear product-market fit evidence
- Major technical unknowns not addressed
- Dependency on unproven technology

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- MVP tested with limited users
- Product-market fit hypothesis but not validated
- Technical risks identified but mitigation underway

**LOW RISK signals:**
- Multiple iterations with real users
- Clear product-market fit signals
- Technical risks understood and mitigated

#### 2. **Team & Execution Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- Key skills missing (technical, business, domain)
- No founder execution history
- Team friction or unclear roles
- Founder distracted or commitment unclear

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- 1-2 key skills to develop
- First-time founder but strong learner
- Team forming, roles clarifying

**LOW RISK signals:**
- Complete team with relevant experience
- Founder has execution track record
- Strong team collaboration and clarity

#### 3. **Market & Competitive Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- Large, well-funded competitors present
- No clear competitive advantage identified
- Market size or adoption uncertain
- Vietnam regulatory barriers not addressed

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- Competitors exist but differentiation identified
- Market size reasonable but not validated
- Some regulatory awareness but path unclear

**LOW RISK signals:**
- Clear competitive advantage and moat
- Large addressable market with growth signals
- Regulatory path clear or low-barrier vertical

#### 4. **Go-to-Market & Capital Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- No customer acquisition strategy
- High cost-to-acquire vs. customer lifetime value
- Burn rate unsustainable, funding runway unclear
- Distribution channel not identified

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- GTM strategy identified but not validated
- Runway 6-12 months with clear next funding step
- Multiple channels being tested

**LOW RISK signals:**
- Proven customer acquisition channels
- Unit economics work or path clear
- 18+ month runway or clear funding path

#### 5. **Vietnam Context Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- No understanding of Vietnam market dynamics
- Regulatory uncertainty or blockers
- No local partnerships or ecosystem awareness
- Payment/logistics infrastructure not addressed

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- Basic Vietnam market research done
- Some regulatory awareness but gaps remain
- Early ecosystem exploration underway

**LOW RISK signals:**
- Deep Vietnam market knowledge demonstrated
- Clear regulatory path and compliance plan
- Local partnerships and ecosystem integration

#### 6. **Data Completeness Risk**

**HIGH RISK signals:**
- 5+ critical data gaps (founder feedback, traction, roadmap, mentors, KPIs)
- Health score cannot be reliably assessed
- Risk judgment relies heavily on assumption

**MEDIUM RISK signals:**
- 2-4 data gaps but key info present
- Health/risk score has some foundation
- Directional judgment possible with caveats

**LOW RISK signals:**
- All critical fields populated
- Health/risk score well-supported
- High-confidence judgment possible

### Risk Level Scoring Logic

```
HIGH RISK if:
  - Any strong high-risk signal present (e.g., no user validation)
  - OR multiple medium signals + gaps
  - OR 5+ data completeness issues

MEDIUM RISK if:
  - 1-2 medium signals + low-risk signals elsewhere
  - OR some validation but gaps remain
  - OR 2-4 missing critical fields

LOW RISK if:
  - Primarily low-risk signals across dimensions
  - AND clear validation evidence
  - AND complete data profile
```

## Using Scores for Decisions

### Mentor Allocation

**High-Risk Startups (Score < 60)** need **intensive validation support**:
- Weekly check-ins
- Focus on: Product validation, team building, GTM testing
- Mentors: Domain experts, go-to-market specialists
- Milestones: User feedback loops, revenue/adoption metrics

**Medium-Risk Startups (Score 60-75)** need **growth fundamentals support**:
- Bi-weekly check-ins
- Focus on: Scaling what works, market expansion, fundraising
- Mentors: Growth specialists, market strategists
- Milestones: Unit economics, channel optimization

**Low-Risk Startups (Score > 75)** need **scaling execution support**:
- Monthly check-ins
- Focus on: Market expansion, team building for scale
- Mentors: Executive coaches, venture advisors
- Milestones: Revenue growth, geographic expansion

### Data Collection Priorities

If a startup shows **data gaps** (missing in rubric fields):

1. **Priority 1 â€” Critical**: Traction, revenue, customer feedback
2. **Priority 2 â€” Important**: Team skills, GTM clarity, Vietnam market research
3. **Priority 3 â€” Nice-to-have**: Advanced metrics, competitive analysis

Request from founder in order of priority. Re-assess health/risk after each data collection round.

### Risk Review Process

**High-risk startups** need **focused SGA review**:

```
1. Schedule validation review (1-2 weeks out)
2. Prepare brief: Health score, risk signals, missing data
3. Founder meeting: Ask for evidence on gaps
4. Update score + risk assessment
5. Mentor recommendation
6. Next milestone clarity
```

**Medium-risk startups** need **standard check-ins**:

```
1. Bi-weekly mentor feedback collection
2. Monthly founder progress update
3. Quarterly deep assessment
```

## Appendix: Framework Weights Rationale

**Why Traction (25%) and Team (20%) are weighted heavily:**
- Traction = market validation (most concrete proof)
- Team = execution capability (can founder actually ship?)

**Why GTM (18%) and Product (20%) are crucial:**
- GTM = customer acquisition (proves business model)
- Product = market fit (proves founder can build)

**Why Vietnam context (12%) matters:**
- Regulatory environment unique
- Payment infrastructure varies
- Market behavior differs from Western markets
- Local partnerships critical for success

**Why business model (5%) is small but important:**
- If traction is there, model likely works
- But clarity prevents scaling issues later
